# Youth Voices for Antimicrobial Awareness (YVAA)

**A youth-led initiative promoting antimicrobial resistance awareness and responsible antimicrobial use.**

## About YVAA

Youth Voices for Antimicrobial Awareness (YVAA) is an independent youth-led initiative founded in 2026 by a PharmD graduate from Pakistan. It aims to improve public understanding of antimicrobial resistance (AMR), promote responsible antibiotic use and encourage youth participation in antimicrobial stewardship.

## Mission

To engage students and young people in addressing antimicrobial resistance through accessible educational resources, awareness activities and public engagement.

## Key Activities

- Development of educational materials on AMR and antibiotic misuse.
- Awareness and knowledge-sharing activities.
- Survey-based assessment of AMR awareness and understanding.
- Encouraging youth participation in global health and antimicrobial stewardship.

## Project Documentation

This repository brings together the initiative's educational resources, survey findings, activities and progress reports.
